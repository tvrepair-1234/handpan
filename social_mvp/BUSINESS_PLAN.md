# BUSINESS PLAN

## Vision
Build a community-first social platform around interests and local/shared identity, then expand to short-form video once engagement loops are strong.

## Product strategy
### Phase 1 (MVP)
- Free app.
- Success metrics: DAU/WAU, retention (D1/D7/D30), posts per active user, comment rate, community join rate.

### Phase 2
- Premium accounts.
- Profile customization.
- Verified badge.
- Advanced creator/community analytics.

### Phase 3
- Boosted posts.
- Paid promotion for creators, communities, local businesses.

### Phase 4
- Creator subscriptions.
- Tips/donations.
- Platform commission between 5% and 15%.

### Phase 5
- Ads introduced only after strong user activity and retention quality.

## Go-to-market outline
- Start with 1-2 high-intent interest niches.
- Seed communities with moderators and creator partnerships.
- Incentivize first posts and first comments.
- Weekly retention experiments (onboarding, notification timing, content prompts).

## Future roadmap (post community-product fit)
- Short video uploads.
- Vertical video feed.
- Creator tools.
- Trending video feed.
- Recommendation system.
- Video monetization.

## Financial approach
- Keep infrastructure lean with managed backend (Supabase).
- Track CAC vs. retained active users before scaling paid acquisition.
- Stage monetization to avoid harming early retention.
